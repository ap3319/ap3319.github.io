Final Year Project

Connect 4 Playing Robotic Arm

A robotic arm that plays Connect 4, designed as an outreach demonstrator. Two cameras let it see the board and the discs, and a vacuum gripper lets it place its own pieces.

Sec 01

How It Works

Seeing the board

ArUco markers fixed to the board give its position and orientation relative to the camera, through a perspective-n-point solve. Each camera is calibrated beforehand with ChArUco boards so that this estimate is accurate. Discs are found by colour segmentation in HSV space, and the filament for the 3D-printed discs was chosen to make that segmentation reliable.

Moving the arm

Visual servoing uses the position of the target in the camera image to drive the base rotation and the reach of the arm.

Handling the discs

A vacuum gripper picks up each disc and releases it above the chosen column.
